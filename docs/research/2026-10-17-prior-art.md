## Where Whisk already leads

- **Ecosystems.** No Stellar or Starknet adapter exists on npm for App Kit. Its Stellar
  entry has `cctp: null`, and Starknet does not appear at all. Of the four widgets, only
  LI.FI has Stellar and none has Starknet.
- **Fee maths.** Bridge Kit parses `minimumFee` with
  `BigInt(Math.round(Number.parseFloat(minimumFee) * 100))` (`provider-cctp-v2` ~10715),
  and Circle's own doc snippets round the burn fee down. Whisk's `parseFeeBps` and
  ceiling `computeBurnFee` (#118) are exact.
- **No telemetry, no fee skim.** App Kit sends telemetry to api.circle.com by default,
  with `sourceAddress`, `txHash` and `amountIn` among the fields, and routes EVM burns
  through Circle's bridge contract, which keeps 10% of integrator fees.
- **Footprint.** `adapter-viem-v2` is 939 KB raw and pulls in `@solana/web3.js` and
  ethers v5 pieces; every Circle package bundles its own copy of their internal core.
  Four packages set a global `Buffer` polyfill while declaring `sideEffects: false`.

## Defects in Whisk that the reading surfaced

Each one is confirmed against the branch. The issue number is given where one exists.

1. **Re-attest cannot work** (#66). `iris/reattest.ts` goes through `fetchIrisJson`,
   which always sends `GET` (`iris/fetch.ts:61`), and reads `{ messages }`. Circle
   requires `POST /v2/reattest/{nonce}` and answers `{ message, nonce }`; the new
   attestation is then polled from `/v2/messages`.
2. **A 429 is retried after milliseconds.** Iris blocks the whole IP for 5 minutes after
   a 429, and every widget user behind that IP shares the limit. Plan for 35 requests a
   second; the docs give 35 in one place and 40 in another.
3. **Polling gives up at 15 minutes** (`iris/messages.ts:76`). Standard transfers take
   15 to 19 minutes from Ethereum and OP-stack chains, 2 to 4 hours from Starknet and
   6 to 32 hours from Linea. A Fast transfer that falls back to Standard also runs long.
4. **Iris states are mistyped.** Iris returns only `complete` and
   `pending_confirmations`; the `failed` status in `IrisAttestationStatus` never arrives,
   and `delayReason` (`insufficient_fee`, `amount_above_max`,
   `insufficient_allowance_available`) is a reason for delay, not a failure. A forwarded
   transfer is done at `forwardState` `CONFIRMED` with a `forwardTxHash`, not only
   `COMPLETE` (`iris/messages.ts:81`, `engine/bridge/execute.ts:362`).
5. **In-flight records are written too late and expire.** `useWhisk` saves a record only
   after `send` returns a failure, so a tab closed between burn and mint loses the
   transfer. Records expire after 48 hours (`recovery/persistence.ts:28`), which can
   drop a burned, unminted transfer (#86).
6. **No Fast fee buffer.** If the Fast fee moves up between quote and burn, Circle settles
   the transfer as Standard without telling anyone. Circle recommends a 10 to 20% buffer;
   Bridge Kit adds 10% and uses `forwardFee.high`.
7. **Arc's gas floor is missing.** Arc drops any transaction with `maxFeePerGas` under
   20 gwei: no receipt, never mined. Gas is paid from the same USDC balance, so a "max"
   send must hold some back.

## Findings by area

### Route and speed (Step 5)

- Circle marks Fast as N/A on Arc, Avalanche, Polygon, Sei, Sonic, Monad, XDC,
  HyperEVM and Stellar because Standard already attests in seconds there (about 0.5 s on
  Arc). `cctp-go` picks Standard on those chains on its own (`cctp-go/chains.go:385-394`).
  Bridge Kit has no such rule and always asks for Fast.
- Network comes from the source chain everywhere it is done well: Bridge Kit derives
  the Iris host and the testnet/mainnet mismatch check from the source chain's
  `isTestnet`.
- Bridge Kit polls Standard transfers from chains that need more than 20 confirmations
  every 30 s instead of every 2 s.
- **Standard can still cost.** Some `TokenMessengerV2` deployments enforce a minimum
  fee on Standard (the docs list Sei). `maxFee = 0` reverts there, so call
  `getMinFeeAmount(amount)` first. It reverts on older deployments, so treat a revert
  as "no minimum".

### Quotes bound to execution (Step 6)

- **Circle signs quotes.** `POST /v2/quote/burn/usdc/{src}/{dst}` returns a
  `signedQuote` for the Fast fee, the Forwarding fee or both, priced in USDC or native
  gas. `TokenMessengerWithFees.depositForBurnWithFees` takes it on chain. The quote is
  bound to amount, destination domain and burn token, plus destination caller and hook
  data for forwarding; any difference reverts. It expires in about 2 minutes and the
  recipient gets the full amount. Only `upfrontFeeSource` chains in the registry offer
  it. Source: https://developers.circle.com/cctp/concepts/upfront-fee-quotes
- Bridge Kit binds quotes only on that path (`bridge-kit` ~14520-14860): it pins hook
  data, recipient and destination caller, refreshes within 30 s of expiry, has Iris
  check the exact ABI arguments after approvals, and checks expiry, signed quote, fee
  total and nonce before the burn. Its ordinary `estimate()` and `burn()` each fetch
  fees again, with no expiry.
- None of the four widgets binds the quote the user saw to what is signed. Wormhole
  refetches 5 s before expiry, paused while the tab is hidden or a transfer runs, but
  then sends the new quote silently. LI.FI accepts a new quote within slippage without
  asking; its own comment says this can nearly double slippage.
- **Gas quoted is gas sent.** Bridge Kit shares `BRIDGE_STEP_GAS_FLOORS_EVM` (approve
  150k, burn 500k, mint 400k) between estimate and execute and submits
  `max(estimate * 1.25, floor)`.

### Recovery and the in-flight store (Step 7)

- **Possible double burn in Bridge Kit.** It stores the burn hash, then waits for the
  receipt. If the wait throws, the step goes to `error`, and the retry rule re-runs the
  burn without first checking the stored hash (~11570, ~15577). Whisk must persist the
  hash as soon as the wallet returns it and look it up on resume, never sign again.
- **Already-minted checks.** Bridge Kit never checks. The contracts expose it:
  - EVM: `usedNonces(bytes32)` returns 1;
  - Solana: the PDA `["used_nonce", nonce]` exists (Whisk's seed is right; Circle's
    retry-failed-mint doc still shows the V1 seeds);
  - Stellar: `is_nonce_used`, error 6908;
  - Starknet: `is_nonce_used(u256)`.
- **Match by nonce.** Bridge Kit checks that some message is complete, then returns
  `messages[0]`.
- **Re-attest flow** (Bridge Kit ~12514-12620, ~15373-15420): skip if
  `decodedMessageBody.expirationBlock` is `"0"`; otherwise `POST` with body `{}`, then
  poll `/v2/messages` until `expirationBlock` is `"0"`. Expiry is counted in the
  destination chain's own height: the L1 block on Arbitrum, EDGE and Plume, the slot on
  Solana, the ledger on Stellar. An expired mint reverts without using the nonce, and
  re-attesting has no deadline.
- **Check the attestation before minting.** Bridge Kit's
  `assertCCTPv2AttestationParams` (~12975) checks domains, finality threshold, sender,
  recipient, amount and burn token. Whisk should add destination caller, `maxFee` and
  hook data.
- **Errors that say what to do next.** `KitError` carries `recoverability:
  RETRYABLE | RESUMABLE | FATAL`. A forwarding failure is `RESUMABLE` and carries the
  message and attestation so the caller can mint by hand.
- **Persistence done right, by parts.** LI.FI saves the whole route on every update
  through pluggable async storage (`SDKStorage`) but wipes it on a schema bump
  (`migrate: () => ({ routes: {} })`). Wormhole keeps at most 3 transfers and its resume
  panel is off by default. Relay and Across save nothing. Whisk should take LI.FI's
  shape with real migrations and no cap or expiry on burned records.
- **Resume by tx hash.** Wormhole `resumeFromTx` and Across `getDeposit`. Bridge Kit's
  `retry()` needs the whole result object.
- **Bounded polling.** None of them has a total limit with backoff. Relay never counts
  `pending` responses and polls forever; Across keeps polling past `fillDeadline`.
  Bridge Kit polls 600 times at 2 s, ignores `Retry-After`, and never passes its
  `AbortSignal` down to the poller.

### Arc (Step 8)

- Native USDC has 18 decimals; the ERC-20 view at `0x3600…` has 6. Bridge Kit always
  scales bridge amounts with 6. It has no gas floor and its balance check ignores that
  gas comes out of the same USDC.
- Arc's own CCTP page uses the V1 four-argument `depositForBurn` against
  `TokenMessengerV2` and relays bytes from the source event. Those bytes carry nonce 0,
  which is always used, so the mint reverts. Do not copy it.

### Stellar and Starknet

- Stellar burns take 7-decimal amounts and the contract truncates to 6. Inbound
  transfers must go through `CctpForwarder`, with both `mintRecipient` and
  `destinationCaller` set to the forwarder, or the funds are stuck for good. Whisk's
  hook encoding and 7/6 helpers match the contracts.
- G and M Stellar recipients need a USDC trustline.
- Iris returns `null` for every address field on Stellar messages; parse the raw
  message instead.
- Starknet tx hashes must be zero-padded to 66 characters before an Iris lookup.

### Widget (Step 9)

- LI.FI portals dialogs into the widget's own container and offers light, dark and
  system themes. Relay and Wormhole portal to `document.body` with no system theme.
  Relay scopes styles with prefixed CSS variables and `@layer`.
- LI.FI maps numeric error codes to text that says where the funds are
  (`getErrorMessage.ts`) and types wallet rejections. Classifying errors by matching
  message text, as Bridge Kit does for expiry, breaks quietly.
- Patterns to avoid: unawaited `mutateAsync`, throws before `try` in click handlers,
  module-level singletons, and a global testnet flag.
- Server rendering: `'use client'` and a client-only gate with a skeleton.

### Tests (Step 10)

- `stellar-cctp/contracts/message-transmitter-v2/src/test_utils.rs:176-233` has full
  message hex with two-signature attestations, one valid and five negative cases.
  They suit Whisk's message parser tests.
- LI.FI's `reload.unit.spec.ts` reloads mid-transfer and resumes. Whisk needs the same
  test against the fake Iris and fake wallets.
- No Circle package ships its tests.

## Decisions for the maintainer

1. **Circle's signed quotes.** Using `depositForBurnWithFees` on `upfrontFeeSource`
   chains locks the fee for about 2 minutes and pays the recipient in full. It adds a
   second burn path and a quote request to Circle per send. The alternative is
   `maxFee` with a buffer on every chain.
2. **Fast fee buffer and forwarder level.** Bridge Kit pays 10% over the Fast fee and
   the `high` forwarder level. A larger buffer costs the payer more; too small a buffer
   drops the transfer to Standard. Unused budget is not refunded: it goes to the
   destination as priority gas.
